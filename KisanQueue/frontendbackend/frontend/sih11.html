Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know my token position in the queue?

Your token and the number of farmers ahead of you update in real time on the Dashboard and "My Turn" section.

When will I receive my produce payment?

Once your produce completes Quality Check and Weighing, payment is triggered via PFMS directly to your bank account within 24 to 48 hours.

What if I am running late for my time slot?

KisanQueue reserves your token order; if you arrive later than the slot, you will be placed at the nearest available opening without losing your registration.
